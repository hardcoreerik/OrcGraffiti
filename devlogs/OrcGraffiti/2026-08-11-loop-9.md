### Scoped, then executed, the pipeline wiring

Earlier in the session this was deliberately deferred as "real work
deserving its own pass." Revisited it properly this loop: `FaceSampler`
and `ImagePaintPipeline` hardcoded `PlanarProjectionSettings` throughout
(4 call sites in `FaceSampler.cpp` alone), and `ImagePaintRequest::projection`
being a concrete type rather than something that could hold cylindrical or
spherical settings meant the projection math landed earlier had no way to
actually reach the sampling loop.

Added `ProjectionSettings = std::variant<Planar, Cylindrical, Spherical>`
to `Projection.hpp` with generic dispatch functions (`project()`,
`alpha_threshold()`, `minimum_coverage()`, `front_face_cosine_threshold()`,
`paint_through()`, `outward_direction()`). The one real design decision:
front-facing testing is NOT the same shape across projection types. Planar
has one fixed camera direction for the whole mesh; a cylindrical or
spherical projector wraps around the object, so there's no single "the
camera is over there" — front-facing has to be evaluated per-face using
the LOCAL outward-radial direction at that face's position.
`outward_direction()` encodes this: constant for planar, computed from
`(p - axis_origin)`'s perpendicular component for cylindrical, and from
`(p - centre)` directly for spherical.

Kept `PlanarProjectionSettings`'s existing shape completely unchanged
(zero breaking changes to its own fields) and instead added matching
`alpha_threshold`/`minimum_coverage`/`front_face_cosine_threshold`/
`paint_through` fields to `CylindricalProjectionSettings` and
`SphericalProjectionSettings` — a bit of field duplication across three
structs, but it keeps each self-contained and avoids a much larger
breaking change to the already-shipped planar struct that GUI/CLI/tests
all depend on.

### The invasive part: fixing every direct field-access call site

Changing `ImagePaintRequest::projection`'s type from a concrete struct to
a variant breaks any code doing `req.projection.width_mm = ...` directly
— variants don't support that. Found and fixed every one:
- `orcgraffiti.cpp`: mutate the local `PlanarProjectionSettings` before
  assigning into `req.projection`, not after.
- `GLGizmoImagePainter.cpp` — **the live GUI paint gizmo** — same pattern,
  two call sites (auto-fit and manual-size branches).
- `test_image_paint_pipeline.cpp`: 6 identical 3-line blocks fixed via one
  `replace_all`, plus one more elaborate block building a
  `PlanarProjectionSettings` locally first.
